from `docs/INTAKE-QUESTIONS.md` once answered. Nothing here is invented.

and clicks one. They are deliberately one per lane so the breadth argument
lands on the first screen. After that the lanes alternate so no two same-lane
projects sit next to each other.

## Index

One line, one number, one thumbnail each. No case study body.

Japanese Motor Market scraper, PICO-8 games, Creative Tech coursework, Resume
Maker, flame thrower, plasma gun, motorized La-Z-Boy, angel pyro sculpture,
bird cage, physical Scrabble work finder, car exhaust, 3D printed car parts,
Ducati S2R, computer case build, e-bike build, Brown Creek Designs, net zero
home, pilot's license, photography highlights, Green Burial pitch, The Real
Estate Collaborative, Classic Motor Market.

Several of these already have image folders in `public/` and were on the
previous version of the site. Reuse them.

Two framing notes carried over from the review:

- The flame thrower and plasma gun stay, but written as engineering: fuel
  delivery, ignition sequencing, failure modes, and the interlocks built so
  they could not fire unintentionally. Leading with the safety system is what
  turns "he built a flamethrower" into "he thinks about what happens when it
  goes wrong."
- Nothing unbuilt goes in the index. An entry with no object and no photo is a
  promise, and a portfolio of promises is worse than a short one.
