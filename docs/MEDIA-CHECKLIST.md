# Media checklist

Five of the eight featured projects have no photography in `public/` yet. The
site cannot ship without them, and no amount of good writing substitutes.

This is the highest-leverage afternoon on the whole project and the one most
likely to get skipped.

## How to shoot

- Plain background, window light, no flash. A garage door open on an overcast
  day is close to perfect.
- Three frames per object minimum: one wide showing the whole thing, one
  mid-build with hands in frame, one detail close enough to see the weld, the
  machining, or the wiring.
- Anything that moves gets a video clip under ten seconds, not a gallery.
- Shoot in landscape for heroes. Portrait crops badly in a grid.
- Do not clean the shop first. Process photos sell harder than staged ones.

## Needed, in priority order

### 1. Digital record player

The single most valuable media on the site. Shoot:

- The whole unit on a table, powered on, album art visible on the round screen
- **Video: the platter spinning with the album art rotating.** Ten seconds.
  This is the hero clip for the entire site.
- Internals: the Pi with the DAC HAT stacked, in the plinth
- The 12V wiring, the fuse, and the buck converter
- The original OEM switches

Also worth doing: redraw the two ASCII architecture diagrams from the README
as proper graphics.

### 2. Engine rebuild

- Bare block on the stand
- Crank and bearings laid out
- The head, valves visible
- Timing marks
- **Video: the first start,** if it exists anywhere. If it does not, say so
  and we write around it.
- Anything with a measuring tool in frame: bore gauge, plastigauge, feeler
  gauges. Measurement photos are what make a rebuild page credible.

### 3. Coleman frame (or the car radio)

- Tacked up in the jig
- **Finished welds close enough to see the beads.** Most people forget this
  and it is the actual proof of skill.
- Loaded on the vehicle, in the field, in use

If the radio wins slot 08 instead: the print revisions lined up in a row,
worst to best, plus the final part installed in the dash. A row of failed
revisions is a genuinely great photograph.

### 4. FSAE

- The part you owned, installed on the car
- CAD screenshots and FEA plots
- The car running at competition

A photo of your part on the car beats any render.

### 5. Phantom typewriter

- **Video, and it is not optional.** Nobody will understand this project from
  a still image. Ten seconds of it doing the thing.
- Internals, whatever the mechanism is

### 6. RISD car design

Four images exist but scans, not photographs, are what this page needs.

- Scan the sketches flat, in even light, not photographed at an angle on a
  desk
- Include the rough ones and the abandoned direction. Process sells harder
  than one clean render.

### 7. LandedTheJob and Japanese Motor Market

Software needs recording, not photographing.

- Screen recording of the full paste-to-resume flow
- Screenshots of the JMM listings page and the admin dashboard
- One chart built from the 20,245 scraped listings. This is the most
  interesting single object either project could produce and the data is
  already on disk.

### 8. About page

One real photograph of Atreyu working on something with his hands. Not a
headshot. It sets the thesis before anyone reads a word.
